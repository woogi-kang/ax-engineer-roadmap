# Research

This directory records the external evidence behind the roadmap's role model, structure, and public cases. It keeps hands-on practice separate from cases reported by outside organizations.

## Documents

- [Public AX case catalog](public-ax-cases.md): compares the workflow, human judgment, reported result, and evidence limits of 10 Korean and 15 international cases.
- [Public case-to-practice table](public-case-application-map.md): shows where questions and controls from all 25 public cases inform 10 existing practices and five new ones.
- [Public AX Engineer role review](ax-engineer-role-review.md): separates the 2026-07-23 baseline from role and training evidence checked on 2026-10-26, and compares internal AX with adjacent responsibilities.
- [Developer and AI Agent roadmap review](roadmap-benchmark-review.md): explains what this project borrows from existing roadmaps and what it adds for AX work.
- [Evidence review for operations and adoption](operating-evidence-review.md): publication and observation dates, samples, and limitations of organizational surveys informing costs, policy compliance, and role-specific acceptance.
- [Source policy](source-policy.md): defines how the repository separates facts, public research, practitioner evidence, and hypotheses.
- [Standards and proposals to review](standards-watchlist.md): separates plans from actual product support and records checks before inclusion in the core curriculum.

## How to read this directory

- `case-studies/` contains cases designed for direct execution or practice in this repository.
- Public cases in this directory are structured references based on official announcements and customer stories from outside organizations.
- A metric reported by a company or vendor is not treated as independently verified when its method and underlying data are unavailable.
- Reusing an idea from a public case still requires new decisions about data, approval, authority, evaluation, stopping, and recovery in the target organization.
