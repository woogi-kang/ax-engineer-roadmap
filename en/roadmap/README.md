## Reading principles

- Look at the actual responsibility, not the job title.
- Distinguish experience using a technology from experience producing an operable outcome.
- Do not judge proficiency by tenure or number of tools.
- Connect every competency to a verifiable artifact or operating record.
- Non-developers and developers do not need the same coding depth, but each must explain the approval, acceptance, implementation, and operating boundary they own.
